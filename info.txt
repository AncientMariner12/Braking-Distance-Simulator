Temperature calculation
    This is a toggle to enable the option to calculate temperature.
    It is off by default, and if it is off, the 4 inputs below are not required.

Ambient Temp
    The outside temperature in the shade, measured in degrees Celsius.

Brake melting point
    The temperature at which the structure of the brake disk fails because of heat. Measured in degrees Celsius.

Brake disk density
    The density of the brake disk, measured in g/cm^3.

Disk Volume
    The total volume of all of the brake disks, measured in cm^3.

Reaction Time
    The reaction time is the time it takes for a human to react to a signal, measured in seconds.
    Includes a button labeled "reaction time test," which will run a reaction time test.

Gravity
    Acceleration due to gravity, measured in m/s^2.
    Includes a dropdown menu with the acceleration due to gravity on different planets.

Velocity
    The speed of the car in a single direction.
    Velocity is the vector form of speed, but because the direction in this case is relative to the car, as long as the car is traveling in a straight line (which the program assumes), both velocity and speed will be identical.

friction Coefficient Brakes
    The coefficient of friction between the brake pads and disks, always between 0 and 1.

Friction Coefficient Regen
    The equivalent coefficient of friction caused by engine braking or regenerative braking in the case of an electric vehicle.
    Always between 0 and 1.

Friction Coefficient Tyres
    The coefficient of friction between the tyres and the road, always between 0 and 1.
    Includes a dropdown with the coefficient of friction for different road surfaces.

Mass
    The mass of the vehicle, measured in kilograms.

Grade
    Used to simulate the car braking on a slope. Grade is a percentage, with negative being downhill and positive being uphill.

Clamp force
    Clamp force is the force between the brake pads and the brake disks, measured in newtons. If this is unknown, use a typical value of 5000 N.

Use multiplier
    Toggle to turn on or off the use of the multiplier that can be used to correlate the program to real-world tests.
