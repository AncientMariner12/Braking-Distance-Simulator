Tempature caculation 
    This is a toggle to enable the option to calculate tempature. 
    It is off by defult and if it is off the 4 inputs below are not reqired.
Ambient Temp
    The outside tempature is the shade in degrees centergrade 
Brake melting point
    The Tempature at which the structure of the brake disk fails because of heat. Measured in degrees centergrade
Brake disk dencity 
    The densicty of the brake disk measured in g/cm^3
Disk Volume
    The Total volume of all of the brake disks in cm^3
Reaction Time 
    The reaction time is the time it takes for a human to react to a Signal, measured in seconds 
    Includes a button laybeled "reaction time test" which will run a reaction time test.
Gravity
    Accelliration due to graverity in m/s^2 
    Includes a drop down menu with the Accelliration due to graverity on different planets 
Velocity
    The speed of the car in a single direction. 
    Velocity is the vector form of speed but because the direction is this case is relative to the car, as long as the car is travelling in a straght line (which the program assumes) both velocity and speed will be indenticle
